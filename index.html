Website restoration

An outdated website quietly sends customers to your competitors.

Most visitors decide in a few seconds. If your site is slow, hard to read on a phone, or doesn't say what you do, they leave. A restoration keeps what works and fixes the rest.

  • Breaks or squashes on phones — rebuilt mobile-first.
  • Slow to load — lighter pages, optimised images.
  • Out-of-date info — fresh copy, prices, hours and photos.
  • No clear next step — obvious call, WhatsApp and booking buttons.
  • Invisible on Google — proper titles, descriptions and business details.

FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

Something else on your mind? Ask on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After a short call you'll get a fixed written quote, and that's the price. No hourly billing surprises.

How long does it take?

A Starter site is usually ready in one to two weeks. Larger sites and restorations take longer, mostly depending on how quickly content and feedback come in. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

Can you fix my existing website instead of starting over?

Often, yes. That's what a restoration is. I'll review your current site first and tell you honestly whether a fix or a rebuild gives you more for your money.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site files are yours. If you ever want to move on, you take everything with you.

I don't have photos or text ready. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I can help write your content and use quality stock photography until you have your own.

Do you only work with businesses in Dominica?

No. I'm based in Dominica, and I work with clients anywhere over email, WhatsApp and video calls.
