Getting here & good to know.

How do I get from the airport?

We arrange a trusted driver from Douglas-Charles Airport. It's about 75 minutes through the island's interior, and worth every bend.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. A tropical breakfast with fruit from our garden, fresh bread and local coffee is served on your porch each morning.

Do I need a car?

Not necessarily. Buses pass at the gate and we can book drivers and guides for day trips.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival when you book direct.
