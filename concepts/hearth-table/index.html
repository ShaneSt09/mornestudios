Our story

Three generations around one stove.

Hearth & Table started as Mama Rose's lunch window on Old Street. Today her grandchildren run the kitchen, still buying from the same farmers and fishermen every morning.

Nothing frozen, nothing rushed. Just honest food you'd be proud to serve at home.

— The Laurent family
